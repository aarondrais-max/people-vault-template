Generates a daily snapshot of what needs your attention — pending decisions, upcoming milestones, active situations.

Your vault structure

What your vault will look like

Your wiki is organised around the pages that matter most to your role.

wiki/
├── reqs/          job requisitions
├── candidates/    active and archived candidates
├── people/        hiring managers, stakeholders
└── patterns/      cross-req recruiting intelligence
reqs/One page per open requisition. Tracks pipeline, HM preferences, blockers, and referral bullets.
candidates/Two-tier: active candidates get full pages with screen notes and flags; archived candidates get lightweight stubs.
people/Hiring managers, stakeholders, and key relationships — interaction history and what they care about in hiring.
patterns/Cross-req intelligence — patterns Claude has learned about your candidates, market, and process.
wiki/
├── people/        managers, employees, exec partners
├── teams/         team health and org structure
├── initiatives/   people programs you’re running
└── decisions/     ER outcomes, coaching commitments
people/Key managers, employees, and executives — relationship notes, interaction history, open issues.
teams/Team health notes, org structure, headcount, and recent changes for each BU you support.
initiatives/People programs and change management projects — goal, stakeholders, status, and next steps.
decisions/ER case outcomes, policy calls, and manager coaching commitments — context, decision, and precedent notes.
wiki/
├── offers/        offer decisions and economics
├── benchmarks/    job family comp ranges and market data
├── cycles/        comp review and equity refresh timelines
└── decisions/     exceptions, precedents, philosophy calls
offers/Offer economics — proposed comp, market reference, decision, exception notes. Not candidate profiles.
benchmarks/Comp ranges by job family and level, data sources, internal equity notes, and refresh schedule.
cycles/Annual review, focal, and equity refresh cycles — timeline, scope, key decisions, and current status.
decisions/Exceptions approved, comp philosophy calls, and equity grants — with rationale and precedent implications.
wiki/
├── projects/      initiatives and programs
├── people/        key stakeholders and vendors
├── decisions/     key decisions and rationale
└── processes/     documented workflows and SOPs
projects/Initiatives and programs you own or contribute to — overview, status, next steps, decisions log.
people/Key stakeholders, vendors, and partners — interaction history and what you need to remember about working with them.
decisions/Key decisions and their rationale — great for onboarding new teammates or revisiting why you did something.
processes/Documented workflows, runbooks, and SOPs — living documents that Claude helps keep current.
Step 3 of 3

Setting up your vault

Follow the steps below to get your vault up and running.

1

Download the vault

Go to github.com/aarondrais-max/people-vault-template, click the green Code button, then Download ZIP. Unzip it somewhere permanent — a good spot is ~/Documents/PeopleVault.

Tip: Don’t leave it in Downloads. The vault grows over time and you want it somewhere you’ll remember.
2

Install Obsidian

Download Obsidian for free from obsidian.md. Open it, choose Open folder as vault, and select the folder you just unzipped. You’ll see all the wiki pages in the left sidebar — this is where your structured notes will live.

Tip: Use Cmd+P to search across all your vault pages instantly.
3

Open Claude Code in your vault folder

You already have Claude Code — it comes with Claude Desktop. Open Terminal (Spotlight → type “Terminal” → Enter) and run:

Terminal
cd ~/Documents/PeopleVault && claude

Claude Code will open inside your vault folder and is ready for the next step.

Note: If you unzipped the vault to a different folder name, adjust the path above to match — e.g. cd ~/Documents/people-vault-template && claude. If you get a "no such file or directory" error, open Finder, right-click your vault folder, hold Option, and choose Copy as Pathname — then replace the path above with what you copied.
4

Run the setup wizard

In Claude Code, paste the following and press Enter:

Please read skills/vault-setup.md and run the setup wizard for me.

Claude will ask you a short series of questions — your role, name, timezone, which tools you have connected (Slack, Gmail, Granola), and how you want your daily brief delivered. Takes about 5 minutes.

When it’s done, Claude will have written your personalised CLAUDE.md and vault.yaml — no manual editing needed.

What Claude asks: role → name & timezone → vault location → connected tools → Slack channels to monitor → brief delivery (Slack DM or local file)
5

Start your first session

You’re set up. Open a new Claude chat at claude.ai and paste this to load your vault context:

Boot the vault

Claude will read your CLAUDE.md, understand your role and current work, and be ready to help. Try a first ask relevant to what you’re working on:

Good first ask: “Help me create a wiki page for the [job title] role I’m hiring for”
Good first ask: “Help me capture key notes from my last 1:1 with [manager name] as a wiki page”
Good first ask: “Help me create a benchmark page for [job family] with our current range”
Good first ask: “Help me create a project page for [initiative you’re running]”
5

Run your first pipeline

Still in Claude Code, type the following and press Enter:

/vault-orchestrator

This ingests from your connected tools (Slack, Gmail, Granola), builds your wiki pages, and generates your first morning brief. The first run takes a few minutes — subsequent runs are faster.

After the run: Open HOT.md in Obsidian — it’ll show your current pipeline state, what needs attention, and what Claude surfaced from your tools.
6

Using skills day-to-day

The skills/ folder has instruction files for specific tasks. In Claude Code, trigger them by name — e.g. /vault-boot to load context, /daily-brief for your morning snapshot. In Claude chat, paste the skill file contents to run the same tasks manually.

See the Skills Reference below for the full list.

6

Schedule the pipeline (optional)

The pipeline can run automatically every morning so your vault is always fresh when you start work. Recommended schedule: full pipeline at 07:08, lighter update at 13:09.

See SETUP-TECH.md in your vault folder for the exact scheduler configuration. Or just run /vault-orchestrator manually in Claude Code whenever you want a refresh.

Skills Reference

What you can ask Claude to do

Each skill is an instruction file in the skills/ folder. Paste it into Claude chat (Standard) or invoke it by name in Claude Code (Full Pipeline).

vault-boot
Both

Load vault context at the start of a new Claude session — tells Claude about your setup, role, and current state.

Trigger:"boot the vault"
vault-orchestrator
Full Pipeline

Run the full pipeline — all ingests, wiki update, and morning brief in sequence.

Trigger:/vault-orchestrator
vault-daily-sync
Both

Update wiki pages from latest signals without running ingests. Good for a quick mid-day refresh.

Trigger:/vault-daily-sync
vault-hot
Both

Regenerate HOT.md — the state snapshot that shows what needs your attention at a glance.

Trigger:/vault-hot
vault-refresh
Both

Quick reload of context from HOT.md after another chat session did work. Keeps Claude in sync.

Trigger:/vault-refresh
vault-lint
Both

Weekly health check — flags stale pages, missing next steps, and items with no recent activity.

Trigger:/vault-lint
daily-brief

Trigger:/daily-brief
process-meeting-note

FAQ

Common questions

Answers to the things people ask most often.

Nothing extra. We already have Claude Enterprise, which covers everything here — including Claude Code. Obsidian is completely free. The Full Pipeline path requires MCP connectors, which are already configured in our environment.

Your vault lives entirely on your local machine. Source files — the raw Slack messages, emails, and meeting notes that the pipeline ingests — never leave your computer. Only the wiki summaries that Claude writes are stored in the vault, also locally.

When you send messages to Claude, our enterprise data governance terms apply. Nothing is used to train models under our Enterprise agreement.

Yes. Run the setup wizard again — open Claude Code in your vault folder and paste: Please read skills/vault-setup.md and run the setup wizard for me. It'll ask for your new role and regenerate your CLAUDE.md in a few minutes. Your existing wiki pages won't be touched.

The vault’s skills and CLAUDE.md are designed to work alongside any existing Claude setup. You can drop the skills folder into an existing Claude Code project, or use just the pieces you want. Nothing here requires a fresh Claude installation.
